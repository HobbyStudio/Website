Scoundrel Card Game

Scoundrel is a roguelike dungeon-crawling card game where survival depends on strategy, resource management, and quick thinking. Your goal is to navigate through a perilous dungeon, manage your health, and defeat monsters using weapons and potions. Every decision matters as you fight your way through dangerous encounters, balancing risk and reward.
