Privacy Policy

ChaosWager — Hobby Studio

Effective date: November 13, 2026.

This policy explains how Hobby Studio handles information when you use ChaosWager, its online game server, and this policy page. ChaosWager is a multiplayer physics game with virtual credits and in-game contracts. These credits are not real money and are not redeemable for cash.

For privacy questions or requests, contact hobbystudio25@gmail.com.

Information We Process

  • Google sign-in: Google provides an account identifier and basic profile information, which can include your name, email address, and profile image. This information is processed to authenticate you. The current game server retains Google's stable account identifier and your chosen ChaosWager username, not your Google email address or profile image.
  • Account and game records: We store your username, virtual-credit balances, wagers, in-game contracts, transaction history, and related timestamps and identifiers so that the server can maintain your account and settle rounds consistently.
  • Session information: The app saves a game-session credential on your device. Persistent server storage contains its hash and expiration, rather than the original credential. Short-lived sign-in requests and browser security cookies are used to complete and protect authentication.
  • Device preferences: The app saves your selected server, username, cached game information, session credentials, and music and click-sound preferences locally on your device.
  • Connection and diagnostic information: Our hosting infrastructure processes network information such as IP addresses and request times. The server records operational information such as connections, usernames, game activity, errors, and aggregate performance metrics to operate and troubleshoot the service.

We do not receive your Google password. Google handles its own sign-in and consent screens. ChaosWager does not request access to your Gmail messages, contacts, Google Drive files, or precise device location.

Guest Access

You may continue as a guest without linking a Google account. Guests receive a temporary identifier and can spectate online rounds, but do not receive a persistent economy account, wallet, betting access, or contracts. Guest connections still involve the network and operational information described above.

How We Use Information

  • Authenticate Google accounts and restore the same game account across devices.
  • Reserve unique usernames and apply account permissions.
  • Maintain virtual balances, execute in-game contracts, settle rounds, and prevent duplicate transactions.
  • Remember device settings, operate multiplayer sessions, investigate errors, and protect the service from abuse.
  • Respond to support and privacy requests.

Google account information is used for authentication and account management, not for advertising, sale to data brokers, or training artificial intelligence models. The current ChaosWager client does not include advertising or third-party behavioral analytics SDKs.

ChaosWager's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Information Visible to Other People

ChaosWager is an online multiplayer game. Usernames, internal game identifiers, current wagers, game outcomes, and some virtual-economy information are visible to other players or through public server status and metrics pages. In particular, the current status page can display connected players' usernames and virtual-credit balances. Do not put sensitive personal information in your username.

Google passwords, Google account identifiers used for account linking, and private game-session credentials are not intended to be displayed in public game views or status pages.

Service Providers and Disclosures

We use Google for authentication and Microsoft Azure for game-server hosting, storage, backups, and operational services. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts; those providers may process connection information when you visit it.

  • Microsoft Privacy Statement

Information is processed by these providers as necessary to deliver their services. It may also be disclosed where required by law or where reasonably necessary to investigate abuse, protect users, or secure the service. We do not sell your Google account information. Hosting and service-provider processing may take place outside your country.

Retention and Security

Game sessions expire after 30 days. Expiration, signing out, uninstalling the app, or clearing device storage does not automatically delete your server account or transaction history. Account and game records remain on the server until they are removed through an operator-managed process.

We may need to retain limited records for security, abuse investigations, transaction integrity, or legal obligations. Backup copies may remain until their applicable retention periods end. We can explain any relevant limits when responding to a deletion request.

The production service uses encrypted HTTPS/WSS connections, restricts guest economy access, and keeps Google OAuth client secrets on the server rather than in the game client. You should keep your device and saved game credentials secure. No internet service or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices and Privacy Requests

  • Use guest spectator access instead of Google sign-in.
  • Edit your username, change audio preferences, or sign out through the game's Profile & Settings section.
  • Review or revoke Google's authorization through your Google Account connections. Revoking that authorization does not by itself delete your ChaosWager records or necessarily end an already-issued game session; sign out in the game as well.
  • Request access, correction, or deletion of account information by emailing hobbystudio25@gmail.com. Depending on your location, you may have additional privacy rights.

Please include your ChaosWager username and describe your request. We may ask you to verify account ownership before acting. Do not send passwords, game-session credentials, or Google OAuth secrets by email.

Children's Privacy

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child has provided personal information through ChaosWager, contact us so that we can review and address the situation.

Changes and Contact

We may update this policy when the service or its data practices change. The updated policy and effective date will be published on this page.

Hobby Studio can be contacted at hobbystudio25@gmail.com.
