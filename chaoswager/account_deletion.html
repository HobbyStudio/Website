Request Account Deletion

ChaosWager — Hobby Studio

Send Your Request

You can request deletion of your ChaosWager account and associated data by emailing hobbystudio25@gmail.com. You do not need to have the game installed or sign in to this website to submit a request.

Email an account-deletion request →

If that link does not open an email app, send an email manually to the address above with the subject ChaosWager account deletion request.

  • Say that you want your ChaosWager account and associated data deleted.
  • Include your ChaosWager username, if you have set one. If you have not set a username or cannot remember it, mention that so support can help identify the account.
  • Send the request and follow the ownership-verification instructions provided by Hobby Studio. Never send a password, game-session credential, or Google OAuth secret.

This is a support-managed request, not instant deletion. Opening this page or preparing an email does not submit a request or delete your account. Hobby Studio will review your request, verify ownership, and explain any additional steps and the expected completion timeframe.

Data Covered by Your Request

The request covers your Google account link, ChaosWager username, saved game sessions, virtual wallet, and associated game, wager, and contract records. Deleted virtual balances and account progress cannot be restored as part of normal gameplay.

We may need to retain limited records for security, fraud prevention, transaction integrity, resolving outstanding wagers or contracts involving other players, or legal obligations. We will explain any applicable retention and its reason when handling your request. Backup copies may remain until their applicable retention periods end.

Deleting a ChaosWager account does not delete your Google account. Signing out, uninstalling ChaosWager, or revoking Google's authorization does not by itself delete your server account or its data.

Guest Access and Other Privacy Requests

Guest spectator sessions do not create persistent economy accounts. Device preferences and cached information can be removed by clearing the app's local data. If you previously used Google sign-in, you can still request deletion of that account through the email pathway above even if you now use guest mode.

You may also contact us to request access to or correction of your account information. Read the ChaosWager Privacy Policy for information about the data we process, service providers, and retention.
